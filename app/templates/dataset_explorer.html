Cross-Domain Thermodynamic & Electro-Mechanical Transfer Mapping

How HVAC operational stress dynamics map into rotating machine degradation modes via the VAE latent manifold:

1. Thermal Delta Dynamics

HVAC Chilled Water Delta (Leaving vs Entering water temp) maps to Spindle Thermal Gradient (Process temp - Air temp), detecting Heat Dissipation Failure (HDF).

2. Electro-Mechanical Power Stress

HVAC Supply Fan Motor Power & Compressor Vibration velocity map to Spindle Torque & Mechanical Vibration Index, identifying Power Failure (PWF) and Overstrain (OSF).

3. Progressive Wear & Restriction

Duct Static Pressure and Air Flow restriction (filter choking) map to cumulative Tool Wear friction and high-frequency acoustic emission rise, identifying Tool Wear Failure (TWF).
