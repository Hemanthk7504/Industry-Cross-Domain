Latent Space Disentanglement

Continuous manifold geometry vs fragmented clusters

Why does VAE outperform deterministic autoencoders? Standard autoencoders create sharp, fragmented latent clusters that cause out-of-distribution hallucinations when transferred. The VAE regularizes the latent space with Kullback-Leibler divergence DKL(qϕ(z|x) || p(z)), establishing a smooth, interpolatable manifold.
